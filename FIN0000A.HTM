Goto's Message
Sagamihara | Segment 10 (1:00), pretaped

The video screen comes on. Tarzan Goto sits at the back of a bus with
his New Japan tour bag on the seat beside him. The tape was made before
he left for Sapporo.

TARZAN GOTO: Sagamihara, I am sorry I am not there. Tonight I work for
New Japan in Sapporo, on television. They pay me, I go. That is
business.

He leans toward the camera.

TARZAN GOTO: But tomorrow I am in Konan, and I am FMW again. Onita, you
have a Soviet for a partner now. Fine. Bring him. Pogo and I will send
you both home on stretchers.

TARZAN GOTO: Pogo knows where I stand. Konan. See you there.

The screen goes black.
