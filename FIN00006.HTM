Frontier Joins the World
Sagamihara | Segment 6 (3:00)

The lights go down and the crowd is already on its feet before the music
starts. Atsushi Onita walks to the ring in his leather jacket, slapping
hands all the way down the aisle, and climbs through the ropes. He holds
the microphone for a long moment and lets the building chant his name.

ATSUSHI ONITA: Sagamihara! Tonight FMW is not a small company anymore.
Tonight we join the world.

The crowd cheers.

ATSUSHI ONITA: New Japan has its big buildings. All Japan has Baba. FMW
has you. We go to the towns they forget. We put on the fights they are
afraid to put on television. Karate, judo, boxing, wrestling. Whatever
style you have, come to FMW and prove it.

ATSUSHI ONITA: And to the men who want to take this company from me...

Mr. Pogo's music cuts him off. Pogo walks onto the stage with his sickle
over his shoulder, Big Titan and Horace Boulder on either side of him.
They do not come down the aisle. Pogo raises the sickle and points it at
the ring.

Onita throws down his jacket and waves them in. Pogo shakes his head and
taps his wrist. Later.

ATSUSHI ONITA: Main event, Pogo! Street fight! No rules! I will be
waiting!

Pogo, Titan and Boulder turn and walk back through the curtain. Onita
stays in the ring, breathing hard, as the crowd chants "FMW!"
