Pogo Finds a New Friend
Sagamihara | Segment 12 (2:00)

Ricky Fuji is still lying in the wreckage of the table. Sambo Asako
crawls over to check on him while Onita, bleeding from the forehead,
argues with the referee on the floor. Mr. Pogo, Big Titan and Horace
Boulder stand over them in the ring.

Kevin Sullivan walks down the aisle. Titan and Boulder step forward to
cut him off, but Pogo holds them back with the sickle. Sullivan climbs
in, looks down at Fuji and smiles. Then he holds out his hand.

Pogo looks at it for a long moment. Then he shakes it. The crowd boos.

A young boy hands Pogo the microphone.

MR. POGO: Goto is not here tonight. Goto is in Sapporo, with New Japan.
New Japan pays Goto, so Goto goes.

He points the sickle at Sullivan.

MR. POGO: This man came from America to be here. He does not go to
Sapporo. He stays with me.

Sullivan takes the microphone.

KEVIN SULLIVAN: Onita, in America they told me you were crazy. I came
to see for myself. I've had your boy Fuji twice tonight. Next time, I
want you.

Sullivan drops the microphone on Fuji's chest. Pogo raises the sickle,
Sullivan raises his arms, and the four of them stand over the bodies as
the music hits.
